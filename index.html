Active Directory User Onboarding Tool

Problem: New-hire onboarding across 4 sites meant manually creating AD accounts, then separately syncing and configuring Microsoft 365 for each employee — a slow, multi-step, error-prone process at 400+ employee scale.

What I built: A PowerShell + WinForms desktop app that automates hybrid onboarding end-to-end: input validation and an interactive OU browser, template-based group copying with automatic exclusion of privileged groups, domain-controller replication polling, Azure AD Connect delta sync over WinRM, Entra ID verification via the Microsoft Graph SDK, M365 license assignment, default group/logon-hours enforcement, and automatic welcome-packet generation.

Tools: PowerShell, WinForms, Active Directory, Azure AD Connect, Microsoft Graph SDK, WinRM

Outcome: Cut new-hire provisioning from ~2 hours of manual, multi-system work to under 15 minutes, and eliminated group-membership configuration errors across 400+ managed accounts. Along the way, tracked down and fixed a cross-subnet WinRM connectivity restriction and an EDR auto-containment issue that were silently blocking remote sync.

PowerShell Account Unlock Automation

Problem: Account lockouts were one of the highest-volume ticket categories on the helpdesk, and a large share came in after hours, forcing users to wait on a full ticket escalation for something Tier 1 could safely resolve directly.

What I built: A PowerShell-based unlock tool that verifies the requester, unlocks the AD account, and automatically writes an audit-log entry — removing the need for a full escalation while preserving a compliance trail.

Tools: PowerShell, Active Directory, Group Policy

Outcome: Cut average lockout resolution time from roughly 10 minutes (including escalation wait) to under a minute, and reduced lockout tickets requiring senior IT involvement by an estimated 70%.

AI Workflow Automation Pipeline (ComfyUI)

Problem: Producing a consistent, print-ready product line by hand — art generation, upscaling, and print layout — doesn't scale for recurring live-selling drops.

What I built: An end-to-end automated image-generation and production pipeline in ComfyUI: 77+ character configurations via LoRA, 17 scene themes, and pose libraries feeding a batch render queue, with automated ESRGAN upscaling and automatic print-sheet imposition (3×3 layout at 5100×6600px) for physical production.

Tools: ComfyUI, Stable Diffusion XL, Python, LoRA fine-tuning, GPU-based rendering (RTX 2070 Super)

Outcome: Replaced a manual, one-off creative process with a repeatable production pipeline supporting an active live-commerce product line — producing 500+ print-ready cards per release drop. The relevant IT/automation skills are the batch processing, pipeline design, and GPU resource management underneath it, not the art itself.
